Admission Process

Your Journey in 6 Simple Steps

We've streamlined the entire admission process to make your transition to studying in India as smooth as possible.

1
Share Your Results
Send us your O-level and A-level results along with your academic documents. We'll review and assess your eligibility.
2
Choose Your Course
Based on your qualifications and interests, we'll help you select the perfect course and university from our partner institutions.
3
Receive Offer Letter
We process your application and you'll receive an official offer letter from the university confirming your admission.
4
Confirm Admission

5

Visa Support

Hassle-Free Visa Process

We simplify the Indian student visa process so you can focus on preparing for your new adventure.

01
Receive Offer Letter
Your university offer letter is the first requirement for your student visa application.
02
Gather Documents
We provide a complete checklist and help you prepare all necessary documents including passport, photos, and financial proof.
03
Apply Online
Complete the online visa application form with our step-by-step guidance and review before submission.
04
Attend Interview
We prepare you for the embassy interview with practice questions and tips to ensure you feel confident.
05
Visa Approved!
Once approved, your visa is stamped and you're ready to travel. We help with travel planning and next steps.

Important: The student visa process typically takes 2-4 weeks. We recommend starting your application at least 6 weeks before your intended travel date to allow sufficient time for processing.
