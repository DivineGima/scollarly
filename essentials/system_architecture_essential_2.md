# Essentials 2: Scollarly v2 refactor (one stage, for PRD_2)

*Drafted 2026-10-31 from the owner's approved design document `docs/designs/scollarly-v2-refactor.md`. This is a new version, not an in-place edit of `system_architecture_essential_1.md` (decision ADR-050). The refactor is one stage, by the owner's instruction.*

## Aim & Outcome

Scollarly connects African students with Indian universities. Version 1 (Stages 0 to 12) is built and live. This file is the whole build for the refactor of that live app: it turns an engineer's admin console of about 22 routes into four tabs per role, makes sign in and create account two clear things, adds a student and a parent onboarding that creates a lead, shows each student their name and the universities that fit their chosen course, adds a notifications page, turns the admin and owner screens into easy-to-read CRM dashboards, redesigns the home page into a landing page, and adds the real logo as the app icon and installed-app splash. It keeps what already works: offline reading and the lead queue, Google-only sign-in, the free-plan CPU design, WAT, no email, no paid service, the brand (light only, blue, black, white) and every rule in `CLAUDE.md`.

**What we are building (one sentence per feature):** F12 hub navigation and page help; F13 landing page with a course finder; F14 separate Sign in and Create account; F15 onboarding for students and parents; F16 personalised student screens; F17 notifications; F18 CRM dashboards; F19 brand assets (logo, icon, splash).

**What we test (summary):** every flow end to end on a phone viewport, online and offline; account confirmation and the absence of any "does this email exist" signal; one lead per finished onboarding even after retries; role separation on every hub; the ordering of universities; the CPU of every new screen on the deployed Worker; the public page budgets; accessibility; and a watched test with real people at the end. Full list in "Tests" below.

## Build Steps

### Working rules

1. This is one stage. Work through the modules in the order listed; a module may start only when the one before it has its tests passing in CI (S13.8 is the exception: it can run alongside any module because it is owner-paced).
2. Use a feature branch for each module and open a merge request. Never push to `main`.
3. Every module has an ID (`S13.1`). The tracker (`tracker_2.md`) records status per module ID, one of `in-progress` or `complete`.
4. Write tests with the module. A module is complete only when its tests pass in CI.
5. Every migration is additive-first (architecture section 14). Migration `0013_refactor_v2.sql` adds and backfills; nothing is dropped.
6. If anything in the architecture or PRD looks wrong or conflicts with what you find, stop and flag it. Do not change the design silently. (Five such points are already listed in the architecture's amendment log for the owner to confirm.)
7. Human actions (legal review, Google OAuth publishing, owner sign-offs, trust content, the blue hex, the course list approval) are recorded as done only when the owner says so.
8. All new text uses translation keys. Nothing is hardcoded. The one WhatsApp helper builds every WhatsApp link.
9. Do not add a paid service, a third-party script, a hotlinked image or a new charting library. Do not change hosting.
10. At the end of the stage, update `tracker_2.md` with the date, this file's name, the architecture version and each module's status.

### Exit criteria (the stage is complete when all hold)

- All CI checks green on each module's merge request: build and Worker size, integration on Postgres, Lighthouse mobile, lint and unit, Playwright.
- The CPU rows in architecture 21.13 are recorded in the tracker, each with warm p50, p95, worst and the number cut at the limit. Every gate passes (warm p95 at most 8 ms, none cut), or the failing row is brought to the owner with the measured value and the options. No exception is assumed.
- A new person who taps Sign in without an account sees the confirm screen, and Back leaves no confirmed Scollarly account. No response before Google returns reveals whether an email has an account.
- Every finished onboarding, including after an offline retry or a second device, produces exactly one lead; a parent's lead carries the child's name and the parent's number; the profile stores `relationship`.
- The Universities tab orders as the owner decided (chosen course first, NAAC grade, A to Z, unknown last); the order is computed on the device; no personal data is in any cached page, shell or the public catalogue.
- Admin and owner each reach each of the four jobs in one tap from Home; every old admin address redirects; owner-only items are absent for admins and refused by their data calls.
- Public first load stays under 200 KB and public JavaScript under 118 KB gzip; the landing page shows no unconfirmed statistic, testimonial or bio.
- The app icon and installed-app splash use the white logo on the owner-confirmed dark neon blue; the website keeps the black logo on white.
- The watched test has run with three real people and its findings are recorded; the owner confirms the stage.
- Items that are human actions (R1 to R8, H4, H5, H11, H19 to H21, E1 to E8) are recorded as done only by the owner's word. Open ones are listed in the tracker; they do not block code complete but do block launch.

### Out of scope for this stage (named so nobody builds them by accident)

Push notifications, email, a fourth role, a notifications table, a new charting library, student document upload, WhatsApp Business API, French launch, per-admin WhatsApp numbers, payment taken from students, any paid service.
